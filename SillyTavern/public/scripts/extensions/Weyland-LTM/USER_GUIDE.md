# What's the brain icon? 🧠

That's **Long-Term Memory (LTM)** — it lets your character actually remember what happened in a chat, even way back near the beginning, without you having to scroll up or remind them yourself.

## The short version

Every so often (or whenever you want), your character quietly writes themselves a memory of what's happened so far — like a diary entry. That memory gets saved and stays with them, so later on they can bring up things you did or said ages ago, even if it's way outside what they'd normally "remember" in a long chat.

## How to use it

- Click the 🧠 brain icon in your quick-reply bar (or the Extensions menu → Long-Term Memory).
- Hit **+ New LTM** to have your character summarize the recent conversation.
- You'll see a little chip pop up in the corner when it's ready — you don't have to sit and wait, keep chatting and check back later.
- Once it's done, read it over. Don't like something? Hit **Reroll** to try again, or just edit the text yourself.
- Hit **Save** and it's locked in — your character will remember it from now on.

## Good to know

- **You can edit anything.** Click a saved memory, tweak the text, save again. It's yours.
- **You can delete or merge memories** — check the boxes next to a couple of old ones and combine them, or clean out ones you don't want anymore.
- **Nothing happens automatically unless you want it to** — by default. When enough has happened since the last memory, a small red **"Time for an LTM?"** banner pops up at the top of the screen — that's your nudge. Tap it to jump straight into making one (and it disappears), or just ignore it — it clears itself after about a minute either way, so it won't sit there nagging you.
- **Want bigger or smaller memories?** In LTM Settings you can set exactly how many messages go into each memory, or leave it on Auto and let it match the reminder pacing.
- **Want it more hands-off?** LTM Settings has an "Auto-LTM" option with two levels:
  - **Semi-Auto** — a memory gets drafted for you automatically once you hit the message count, but it still waits for you to open it and hit Save before it counts. You'll see "Drafting an LTM…" then "LTM ready for approval" up top. If you go a while without checking, more drafts just keep piling up (one per chunk of messages) — nothing gets skipped or overwritten, they're all waiting for you whenever you get to them.
  - **Full-Auto** — memories draft AND save themselves in the background, no review needed. You'll just see a quick "Generating LTM…" banner pop up and disappear.
  - Both are off by default — turn one on in Settings if you want it.
- **This doesn't touch your character's personality or the card itself.** It's just memory, layered on top.

That's really it — click the brain, make a memory, keep chatting. 🐾
